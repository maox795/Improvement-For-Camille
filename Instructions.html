How to make it

Step 1

Wash and drain the blueberries.
Cook over low heat until soft.
Add granulated sugar and cook until thick.
Add the juice of ½ lemon, mix well, and let it cool.

Step 2

Mix crushed Oreos with melted butter.
Pour the mixture into a 6 inch cake pan.
Press it firmly onto the bottom and sides of the pan,
then place it in the freezer.

Step 3

Mix softened cream cheese,
granulated sugar,
whipping cream,
and blueberry sauce until smooth.

Step 4

Soak the gelatin sheets in cold water until soft,
then drain them.
Add the gelatin to hot milk and stir until dissolved.
Pour the milk mixture into the blueberry cream cheese
mixture and mix well.
Pour it into the Oreo crust from Step 2.
Refrigerate for at least 2 hours until set.

Step 5

Warm the outside of the cake pan with a hair dryer,
then carefully remove the cake.
Spread blueberry sauce on top,
add a layer of fresh blueberries,
and finish with powdered sugar.
